02 / Apple Watch

Train from your wrist

Ascend has an Apple Watch companion app designed for use during workouts. Start and complete workout sets from Apple Watch without reaching for your iPhone, and log reps and progress from your wrist while training.

Create and edit workout routines in the iPhone app. Detailed workout history, settings, and progress analysis are also available in the iPhone app.

Apple Watch troubleshooting

Ascend is not showing on Apple Watch

Confirm Ascend is installed and up to date on your iPhone. Open Apple’s Watch app, find Ascend in Available Apps, and install or enable it. Keep both devices updated to supported versions of iOS and watchOS.

Workout is not syncing to Apple Watch

Open Ascend on your iPhone, then open it on Apple Watch. Keep the devices paired, nearby, and connected through Bluetooth or Wi-Fi. The watch mirrors an active workout from the iPhone app.

iPhone and Watch connection seems unavailable

Check that your Apple Watch is paired and connected to the iPhone in Apple’s Watch app. Turn Bluetooth back on if needed, then reopen Ascend on both devices.

Health information is missing

On iPhone, check the Health app and confirm Ascend has permission to read the activity data you want to display. Health access is managed in Apple’s Health settings and can be changed at any time.

Still need help?

Close and reopen Ascend on both devices. If the issue continues, restart your iPhone and Apple Watch, then contact support with your iPhone and watchOS versions.

03 / Apple Health

Your activity, with permission

Ascend can integrate with Apple Health (HealthKit) to show daily fitness activity in the app. When you allow access, Ascend reads steps, heart rate, and active calories.

You control Health permissions in Apple’s Health settings. You can change or remove Ascend’s access there at any time. Ascend does not write data to Apple Health.

Tip

If Health data does not appear, open the Health app on iPhone and review Ascend’s read permissions.

04 / Workout tracking

Record work that adds up

During a workout, log each set with its reps and weight. Use the rest timer between efforts, then complete the session when you are done. Your completed workouts remain available in workout history, and strength progress charts help you see changes across your training.

  • Log sets, reps, and weight for each exercise.
  • Use a rest timer to pace your recovery between sets.
  • Complete workouts to preserve them in your history.
  • Review strength progress charts built from your logged training.

05 / Notifications

Stay on schedule

Enable training-day reminders in Ascend’s notification settings and choose a reminder time. Ascend can also notify you about rest timers, streak risk, weekly summaries, and award unlocks when those options are enabled.

Notification delivery is controlled by iPhone Settings. If notifications are off, allow Ascend in Settings > Notifications > Ascend, then return to Ascend to manage each notification type.

06 / Account

Sign in with Apple

On iPhone, you can create or sign in to an Ascend account with Sign in with Apple. Apple may offer Hide My Email, which shares a private relay address instead of your personal email address.

If you use Hide My Email, keep access to that Apple ID and relay address so account messages can still reach you.

07 / Account & data

You stay in control

To sign out, open More, select your profile, then choose Sign out. The account’s cached data on that device is cleared or separated so another person using the device cannot read it.

To permanently delete an account, open More, select your profile, choose Delete account and data, and confirm. The account and synced data are removed from the live database immediately and cannot be restored. Encrypted infrastructure backups may retain residual copies for up to 30 days before being overwritten.

Guest workouts, recorded before signing in, stay on that device and are removed by uninstalling the app. For deletion without the app, use the account deletion page.

08 / Privacy

Private by design

You can use Ascend without an account; in that case, workout data stays on your device. If you create an account and enable sync, Ascend stores the data needed to provide that service. Ascend does not share your data with advertisers and does not show advertising in the app.
