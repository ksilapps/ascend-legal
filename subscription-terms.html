Ascend includes a free workout tracker, optional Ascend Pro subscriptions, and optional one-time tips.

Ascend Pro subscriptions

Monthly and annual subscriptions unlock the same Ascend Pro features (called Premium in some app screens): cloud backup and restore on supported devices, long-term exercise and muscle trends, detailed training Insights and monthly reviews, Premium Dark appearance, and the Apple Watch companion on iOS with a paired iPhone. Cloud availability depends on platform, cloud account, and service configuration. An iCloud backup is not an Android backup.

Reference US prices are $1.99 per month and $19.99 per year; prices may vary by storefront, currency, taxes, and available offers. The localized price and billing period shown by your store before purchase apply. Annual subscriptions charge the full annual price at purchase. No free trial is advertised unless your store explicitly offers one.

Unless a store offer specifies otherwise, payment is charged to your store account upon purchase confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically for the selected monthly or annual period unless cancelled before renewal. On Apple, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Apple may charge renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel in App Store subscriptions or, for purchases made through Google Play, Google Play subscriptions. See Apple cancellation help or Google Play cancellation help. Cancelling does not delete locally stored workouts. Pro access remains available through the paid period, subject to the store's subscription status and any applicable billing grace period.

If a free trial or introductory offer is available, its eligibility, duration, and subsequent price are shown by the store before confirmation. A trial converts to a paid subscription unless cancelled before its deadline; on Apple, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

Use Restore Purchases in Ascend Settings with the store account used to subscribe. Restoring a purchase restores access; restoring workout data separately requires an available cloud backup and the corresponding cloud account. Uninstalling Ascend, erasing workouts, or deleting an Ascend account does not cancel a subscription. When Pro expires, Pro features lock; locally stored workout history remains available in the free tracker. A subscription does not guarantee transfer between Apple and Google store accounts.

Optional one-time tips

Small, medium and large tips are repeatable consumable in-app purchases. Tips are optional and support Ascend development; they are not charitable donations or tax-deductible contributions. No Ascend account is required. Current tips unlock no feature, content, entitlement, or service. Apple processes payment through its in-app purchase system. The localized price displayed by Apple at purchase time is the price that applies. Apple charges your App Store account only after confirmation.

A completed tip is consumed and provides no ongoing access to restore. Ascend does not offer a "Restore tips" control. An interrupted or pending Apple transaction may still complete through the store; do not start a duplicate purchase to recover it.

What is free

Everything you need to train, with nothing held back by session count. Logging workouts, the rest and workout timers, streaks, awards and XP, personal records and estimated 1RM, light and standard dark appearance and basic colour palettes, as many training days a week as you want, the whole exercise library with its instructions and demonstration videos, and locally stored exercise history and recent 30-day exercise trends. Premium Dark, detailed Insights, long-term trends, cloud backup, and the Apple Watch companion require Pro. No account is required for local workouts.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by the store used to pay. For Apple purchases, request a refund through Apple's refund process. For Google Play purchases, see Google Play refund policies. Eligibility depends on the store, region, and applicable law. Cancelling renewal does not itself request a refund. Nothing in these terms limits mandatory consumer rights.

Privacy

The store used to purchase processes payment. RevenueCat handles subscription validation, entitlement status, and transaction history, as well as optional tips. Ascend never receives or stores your payment-card details. RevenueCat uses an anonymous customer identifier in V2. Earlier signed-in purchases may remain associated with an account. See the privacy policy for what is stored and where.
