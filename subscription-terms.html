Ascend is free to use. Ascend Premium is an optional upgrade. This page explains what it includes, how billing works, and how to cancel.

What is free

Everything you need to train, with nothing held back by session count. Logging workouts, the rest and workout timers, streaks, awards and XP, personal records and estimated 1RM, light and dark themes and the colour palettes, as many training days a week as you want, the whole exercise library with its instructions and demonstration videos, and the last 30 days of exercise history and trends. No account is required and none of it needs a network connection.

What Premium adds

  • Cloud sync, backup, and your workouts restored on every device you sign in to
  • Your exercise history and trends past the free 30 days, however far back your training goes

That is the whole list. If a feature is not named here, it is free, and buying Premium will not change it. We would rather sell you two things you do not already have than eight things you do.

Plans and billing

PlanBilling
MonthlyCharged every month until cancelled
AnnualCharged every year until cancelled

Prices are shown in your local currency on the purchase screen, and that price is the one that applies. Payment is charged to your Apple or Google account at confirmation of purchase.

Renewal and cancellation

  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours of the end of the current period.
  • Cancel any time in your store account settings: Apple · Google. Cancelling stops the next charge; Premium stays active until the period you have already paid for ends.
  • If a free trial is offered, any unused part of it is forfeited when a subscription is purchased.

Restoring a purchase

Reinstalling the app or moving to a new device does not require buying again. Open Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the same store account.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds are requested from them rather than from us: Apple · Google.

If a payment fails

A failed renewal does not lock you out immediately. Premium features keep working while the store retries the charge, and your logged workouts always remain on your device and readable whatever the state of a subscription.

Privacy

Purchases are processed by Apple and Google. Ascend never receives or stores your payment details. See the privacy policy for what is stored and where.
