Ascend is a workout tracker that works offline. You can use the whole app without an account. This policy explains what is stored on your device, what is sent to our servers if you choose to sign in, and what is never collected. For the rules on using the app itself, including exercise and injury disclaimers, see the terms of use.

Using Ascend without an account

If you never sign in, your data never leaves your device. Workouts, plans, settings, and history are stored locally and are not transmitted to us or to anyone else.

What we store if you create an account

Signing in is optional. It exists so your workouts can be backed up and restored on another device. If you sign in, the following is stored on our servers:

Data Why
Email address, and display name if you provide one To identify your account and let you sign in and recover access
Workout plans, categories, and scheduled rest days So your routine is restored when you reinstall or use another device
Workout sessions, completed sets, weights, reps, and session duration So your training history and progress are restored
Exercise history and per-exercise defaults To show your previous performance for each exercise
App settings, including your step and calorie goals So your preferences carry across devices

Health and fitness data

On iOS, Ascend can display step count, heart rate, and active calories from Apple Health. This is off by default and only turned on if you enable it and grant permission in the system prompt.

Apple Health readings are never uploaded. They are read on demand, shown on screen, and are not written to our servers or saved into your synced account data. Your step and calorie goals are settings you choose, and those do sync, but the measurements themselves never leave your device.

You can revoke access at any time in the iPhone Health app or in iOS Settings, or by turning health metrics off inside Ascend.

Signing in with Google or Apple

If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive only your basic account identifier and email address so an account can be created for you. We do not receive your password. Apple provides your name only on the very first sign-in; if it is provided, it is stored as your display name. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we only ever see the relay address Apple generates.

Diagnostics and anonymous usage data

So that crashes and data-loss bugs can be found without waiting for someone to report them, Ascend sends two kinds of anonymous diagnostic information. Both are covered by a single switch in the app under More → Privacy → Share anonymous usage data. Turning it off stops both immediately.

  • Crash reports, through Sentry. A report contains the error, the place in our code it came from, and the app and device version. It does not include your name, email, account identifier, or any health reading.
  • Anonymous app activity, through PostHog. This is a fixed list of events: the app being opened, a workout being started or completed, a set being logged, and four failure events — a failed save, a failed sync, settings falling back to defaults, and a screen failing to open. Each event carries only counts, durations, the weekday of the plan, and the workout category.

Neither service is told who you are. We never send your account identifier, email address, or display name to either of them, so the activity recorded is not linked to your account. The set of fields an event is allowed to contain is fixed in the app's source code and anything outside it is dropped before it is sent. Nothing you type — exercise names you add, notes, goals — and no Apple Health reading is ever included. There is no advertising identifier, no cross-app tracking, and no session or screen recording.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers, and there is no advertising in the app.
  • We do not use advertising or attribution SDKs, and the diagnostics described above are not used for advertising.
  • We do not build advertising profiles or track you across other apps and websites.
  • We do not log authentication tokens or health information.

Who processes your data

Account and sync data is stored with Supabase, which provides our database and authentication hosting. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and access is restricted so that each account can only read and write its own rows. Your sign-in session is kept in the secure storage provided by your operating system (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).

Anonymous diagnostics are processed by Sentry (crash reports) and PostHog (app activity), only while the switch described above is on.

Your choices and rights

  • Use the app without an account. Nothing is uploaded except the anonymous diagnostics described above, which you can turn off.
  • Turn off anonymous usage data. More → Privacy → Share anonymous usage data. Crash reports and app-activity events both stop.
  • Sign out. The account's cached data on that device is cleared or partitioned so another person using the device cannot read it.
  • Delete your account. Available in the app under More → profile → Delete account and data, and also by email. See the account deletion page.
  • Access or correct your data. Email us and we will help.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal data, such as access, correction, deletion, and portability. Contact us to exercise them.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Account data is access-controlled so each account can only read and write its own rows, and your sign-in session is kept in your operating system's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) rather than in plain text. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we do not store passwords ourselves — authentication is handled by Supabase, Google, or Apple.

Data retention

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Deletion removes your records from the live database immediately. Encrypted infrastructure backups may hold residual copies for up to 30 days before being overwritten.

Children

Ascend is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
